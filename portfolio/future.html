Garage Inventory System

I’d like to design and develop a program to help manage and track the contents of my garage. The system would use a barcode scanner to check items in and out, streamlining organization and inventory control. Tools, extension cords, bits, attachments, and similar items would be tracked with exact quantities, ensuring I always know what I have and what’s missing. Consumable materials, like screws or nails, would be scanned out by the pack and tracked as either unopened or opened, providing a practical level of detail without the need for exact counts.
